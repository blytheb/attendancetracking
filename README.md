Key Features Covered:
User Roles & Authentication – Admin, Teacher, and Student roles with secure login/register
Student & Grade Management – Add, update, and assign students to grades
Attendance Tracking – Mark attendance (Present, Absent, Sick, Other) with modal for reasons
Advanced Filtering – Search and filter attendance records by month & grade
Livewire-Powered Components – Real-time attendance updates with interactive UI
Automated Notifications & Reports – Send daily & weekly attendance reports via email
Export Attendance Data – Generate CSV & PDF reports for record-keeping
Dashboard & Analytics – Visualize attendance trends with charts and statistics
